## What Swifty Does And Collects
We do not collect personal information.

## Automatic Data
Local logs may exist if the user enables debugging mode.

## Local Storage
Local data such as cache and playlists is stored on the user’s device and only if the user chooses to keep it.

## Third-Party Services
Third-party services may receive limited data needed to provide features, including Discord Rich Presence, MusicBrainz, LRCLIB, and Discogs.
Data shared with third parties is subject to their own privacy policies.

## How Data is Shared
Required metadata may be shared with those services so they can perform their function.
Data shared with third parties is subject to their own privacy policies.
Users can opt-out of using third-party services in the apps settings.

## How Data is Used
We do not collect data.

## Retention
Local data from Swifty remains on the user’s computer until the user manually deletes it. Retention of data shared with third-party services depends on each service’s privacy policy.
## User Rights  
Users may delete any data stored by Swifty in the cache folder. If data is shared with third-party services, any rights over that data depend on the relevant service’s privacy policy. Swifty does not collect personal information directly from users.

## Children’s Privacy  
Swifty is intended for users of all ages. Swifty does not intentionally collect personal information from children or adults. Any songs imported into Swifty and any lyric content are processed through third-party services and are not linked to users by Swifty.

## Changes to This Policy  
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be posted in the app, and continued use of Swifty after the update means you accept the revised Privacy Policy.
